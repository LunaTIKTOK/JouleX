Flexibility scheduler — the orchestrator

The real workload-scheduling algorithm (joulex/orchestrator.py, ported 1:1 and parity-tested): 24 deferrable GPU jobs over the 14-day SYNTHETIC series, 1.0 MW facility cap. It compares run-at-arrival (fixed), earliest-deadline-first greedy, and the orchestrator (greedy + most-constrained-first, each improved by local search). The verdict bar is the production one: GO iff ≥5% better than greedy net of coordination cost, SLA 100%, and 100% of decisions audited.

HCS audit log — local mode

Onboarding path — what has to happen before real suppliers plug in

  1. AESO written permission for commercial use of its data (there is no open-data licence; the sandbox is non-commercial research).
  2. Market participation: register as an AESO pool participant, or be appointed agent of a registered pool participant (ISO Rule 201.2) — about 3 months.
  3. Bilateral outreach to Alberta power suppliers and grid-connected data centres (CAL-3/CoreWeave first), with real flexibility contracts and settlement terms.
  4. Mainnet HCS anchoring (owner decision) and a durable, monitored deployment — the sandbox's local log becomes a Hedera-anchored audit trail.
  5. Real liquidity: counterparties posting real bids/offers. Until then, every price here is a paper LMSR quote, not a market.

Market context (verified Nov 11, 2026, The Block): Apex Group — $3.5T under administration — joined the Hedera Council to explore tokenization and settlement. Institutional tokenization is moving from experiment to adoption; it does not change anything in this sandbox.
